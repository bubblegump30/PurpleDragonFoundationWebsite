# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

Purple Dragon Foundation welcomes respectful participation regardless of age,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, appearance,
race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or level of experience.
This applies to this repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
project-managed spaces, and when representing the project publicly.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and patient with different experience levels.
- Critique ideas and code; do not attack people.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licensing, and requests to stop unwanted contact.
- Keep discussion relevant and make room for other participants.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, personal
attacks, doxxing, impersonation, spam, and deliberate disruption are unacceptable.
Do not post credentials, private messages, or another person's personal data.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to **kyle@purpledragonfoundationltd.xyz**. Include
links or a brief description and tell us any immediate privacy concern.
Do not repeat sensitive evidence in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed
with care, and identifying information will be limited to people who need it
to respond. Absolute confidentiality cannot be promised.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, request corrections, limit
participation, or temporarily or permanently exclude a participant. The response
will consider severity, repetition, and the safety of the community. Serious
conduct may lead to immediate exclusion without an earlier warning.

Questions or requests to reconsider a moderation decision may be sent to the
same email address. Good-faith reporting must not result in retaliation.
